# freeresumefrfr: Vision

## Why This Exists

You've been there. You spend 45 minutes filling out your resume on some slick website, hit "Download PDF," and then, surprise, you need to pay $2.95/week (billed annually) to get the file you just made. With your own information. That you typed.

That's not a product. That's a hostage situation.

**freeresumefrfr** exists because downloading your own resume should be free. Full stop. No trial. No watermark. No "upgrade to remove branding." No account required. frfr.

---

## Brand Voice

- **Confident, not cocky.** We're right about this and we know it.
- **Funny, not try-hard.** The Gen Z energy is real but it's not performed. Keep copy punchy.
- **Roast the competition, don't obsess over them.** One good jab > paragraph of shade.
- **Substance over vibes.** The product has to actually work. The jokes land harder when the tool is legit.

### What we say:
- "Free. frfr."
- "No paywall. No watermark. No cap."
- "Your resume. Your file. Yours."
- "We're not going to charge you to download your own words."

### What we don't say:
- "Premium" (unless it's in a punchline)
- "Upgrade"
- "Unlock"
- Anything that implies features are locked

---

## Core Promises

1. **Download is always free.** The PDF button is never behind a gate.
2. **No account needed.** Your data stays in your browser.
3. **No watermark.** Your resume looks like your resume.
4. **No data selling.** We're not monetizing your job search anxiety.

---

## How We Stay Free

We don't need to be profitable on day one. Options down the road:
- Optional accounts for cloud sync (truly optional, not a soft paywall)
- GitHub sponsors / open source donations
- Referral partnerships with job boards (disclosed, never required)
- Nothing that touches the core download flow

---

## The Anti-Paywall Manifesto (short version)

Resume builders that lock downloads are extracting value from people who are often unemployed, stressed, and time-crunched. It's a dark pattern aimed at the most vulnerable moment in a person's professional life. We think that's messed up. So we built this.

You should be able to make a clean resume and get your file. That's the whole deal.
